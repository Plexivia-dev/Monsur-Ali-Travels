# Visa & Work Permit System Specification & Requirements Summary

## Overview
A tailored workflow management system for tracking Work Permit and Visa Processing across specific countries Greece, North Macedonia, and India (BSF  NDR Processing).

---

## 1. Supported Countries  Modules
1. Greece (Work Permit)
2. North Macedonia (Work Permit)
3. Indian Visa (BSF  NDR Processing)

---

## 2. File Processing Lifecycle  Statuses
Each client file progresses through clear, sequential stages

1. Entry (এন্ট্রি)
   - Passport and basic information received and registered in the system (no active processing yet).
2. Processing (প্রসেসিং)
   - LawyerAdvocate actively starts working on the legal file.
3. Approved  Offer Letter (অনুমোদিত)
   - Work permit  official offer letter is approved.
4. Submitted to BSF  Embassy (জমা সম্পন্ন)
   - IndianBSFEmbassy file submission complete.
5. Passport  Visa Delivered (কমপ্লিট)
   - Final passport and visa handed over to client. File is completed and locked from edits.

---

## 3. Client & Document Requirements
- Internal Worker Portal Only No client login required. Help desk  workers look up clients instantly via Name or Passport No.
- Checklist & Reminders
  - 2x2 Photo
  - ElectricityUtility Bill (কারেন্ট বিল)
  - NID Card copy
  - LandAsset documents (জমির কাগজ)
  - Follow-up call reminders for missing documents
- Bulk Import  Legacy Migration Support easy bulk entry for the ~250+ existing active client files.

---

## 4. Payment & Due Tracking (৩ ধাপের পেমেন্ট ও বকেয়া হিসাব)
Payments are tracked in 3 distinct milestones + manual Due field

1. Step 1 Advance payment at File Submission.
2. Step 2 Payment upon Offer Letter  Work Permit Approval.
3. Step 3 Final payment upon Visa Delivery.
4. Due Management (বকেয়া ট্র্যাকিং) Explicit tracking for pendingdue amounts with total company due reporting.

---

## 5. Invoicing & Tax Compliance
- Printable client receipts  invoices.
- Yearly income, expense, and tax calculation reports for government compliance.
- Strict data privacy Only essential contactaddress fields stored, no sensitive personal licenses or unnecessary details.
